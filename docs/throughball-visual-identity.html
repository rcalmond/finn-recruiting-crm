The mark & wordmark

Throughball.

The ball (filled dot), the weighted pass-curve, the arrowhead — plus the runner's dotted path converging to meet it. Two paths meeting at the right spot: literally a through ball, literally what Regista does (its pass meeting the kid's run). Drawn, not geometric — rounded caps, organic curve, the tactics-notebook hand. The arrowhead alone works as the app icon at small sizes. The wordmark is the house bold-italic with a trailing period carrying the accent — the smallest ownable signature, repeated on phase names and section headers.

Data-semantic colors are exempt. Recency dots (HOT red / ACTIVE teal / COOLING amber / COLD gray / PROSPECTING outline / DECLINED muted), tier chips (A green / B blue / C amber / Nope gray), and category stripes encode data, not brand chrome — they keep their own taxonomy and stay legible. The brand palette governs chrome; the data rainbow governs meaning. Different layers, different jobs.

The four phases are numbered acts, not four color-coded worlds. The ghost numeral deepens through the journey — faint parchment-tone (01) → warm tan (02) → green (03) → full ink (04) — rebuilding the "warming ladder" as escalating weight inside one palette. This is the fix for color-anarchy: momentum shown through depth, not hue.

Throughball vs. Regista — who gets named where

Throughball is the place. Regista is the mind.

The test: does this require an opinion about a specific coach interaction? No → Throughball (organization, structure, the record). Yes → Regista (reading meaning, forming a judgment, deciding what matters).
